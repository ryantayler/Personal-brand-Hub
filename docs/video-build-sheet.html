4 · before you record
Your hook answers four things, fast
  1. Who is it for. An owner knows in the first line this is for them.
  2. Why you. The proof that earns you the right to say it.
  3. Where it is going. A reason to stay to the end.
  4. One thing worth knowing. The single takeaway they leave with.
If the proof is real, it is good enough.
