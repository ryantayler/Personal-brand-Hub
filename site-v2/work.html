Nobody delivers one

A room that holds thousands of people needs staging, rig, screen, audio, vision, comms, crew, transport, and a project manager holding the timeline together. Every one of those is a discipline with people who have spent a career getting good at it.

That's where the whole thesis came from. I've never seen a business get better at all of them at once. I've seen plenty get faster by working with the ones who already are.
