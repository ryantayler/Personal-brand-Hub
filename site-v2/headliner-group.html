Cue 02  ·  Build  ·  What the group is

A group of the best
at each piece

Headliner Group holds my ownership in the live events and production businesses I partner with. It's a holding company and a management company, and it exists so that growth, ventures and products have somewhere to be built from.

The structure is deliberately simple. I partner with a founder, the founder keeps running the business they're already great at, and the group sits alongside them holding a position and backing what happens next. Nobody gets replaced. The people who built the business keep building it.

It's the partnership thesis enacted one layer up. Find the expert who is best at each piece, partner in, and the expert keeps running it.

Cue 04  ·  Build  ·  How it works

Four steps, and
no surprises

01

The conversation

It starts with an email. A conversation about the business, what's working, what's capped, and what the next few years are meant to look like.

02

The fit

Founder led, Australian, live events and production or adjacent to it. Not every business is a fit, and I say so early rather than late.

03

The structure

Partnering or investing, and giving you the option to exit. The shape depends on what the founder actually wants out of it.

04

The growth

New ventures, partnerships, products and optimisation. The founder keeps running the business they're already great at.
