The CRAFT Method™

My Idea Crafting Framework™

Capture, Refine, Assess,
Finalise, Transfer

The system I use to turn a thought into a finished asset. Five steps in a fixed order, and a way of naming the places an idea lives while it's being built.

C

Capture

As soon as a thought arrives it goes down, before it disappears.

R

Refine

Build on it over weeks, adding to it and structuring it until it stands on its own.

A

Assess

Scrutinise it, test it, show it to people who'll be honest, and be willing to end it here.

F

Finalise

Turn it into something tangible and public ready, in the medium that suits it.

T

Transfer

Send it into the world, then archive the note and store the finished asset.

Own

Brain RAM™

Working memory. Fast and creative, and it holds nothing for long.

Own

Digital RAM™

The notes system. Where thoughts are captured and worked on until they become ideas.

Own

Hard Storage™

The long term archive. Where finished assets live once they're out in the world.

Cue 03  ·  Teach  ·  The point of view

Partnerships beat doing
everything in house

Teach · The belief

Most event businesses try to do everything themselves. Their own AV, their own crew, their own venue if they can swing it. It protects the margin and caps everything else, because nobody is the best at all of it. I believe you grow faster by partnering with people who are the best at their piece. You give up a clip of the ticket. You get back quality, reputation, and the time to be great at the thing you're actually great at.
